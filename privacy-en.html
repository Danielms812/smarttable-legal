Privacy Policy

Smart Table — Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Table is a mobile application developed by Daniel Martínez Salinas that allows users to control motorized standing desks via a direct Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) connection.

This privacy policy describes how the application handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about data practices.

2. Data We Collect

Smart Table does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

The application works entirely locally on your device:

  • There are no cloud servers or Internet connections.
  • No user accounts are created.
  • No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

3. Information Stored Locally

The application stores the following information exclusively on your device:

  • Paired device identifiers: the serial code and Bluetooth MAC address of each paired desk.
  • Custom names: names you assign to your desks (e.g., "Office desk").
  • Preferences: movement presets and application settings.

This data is stored only in the device's internal storage (SharedPreferences) and is never sent to any server. Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all this data.

4. Device Permissions

Smart Table requests the following permissions, all for local functionality only:

  • Bluetooth: required to connect to and control your standing desk. Communication is direct device-to-device, with no intermediaries.
  • Camera: used exclusively to scan the QR code on your desk during the pairing process. No photos are taken and no video is recorded.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used exclusively for the voice command feature ("raise", "lower", "stop"). Voice processing is performed locally on your device. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. The application does not contain advertising, analytics, or social media SDKs.

6. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, data security risks are minimal. The Bluetooth communication between the application and the desk uses the standard BLE security mechanisms provided by the operating system.

7. Children's Privacy

Smart Table is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from minors. The application controls motorized equipment and should be used by adults only.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with the corresponding update date.
